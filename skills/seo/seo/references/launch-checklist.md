# Launch checklist for a new site

Top to bottom. Content work starts after step 5 is green.

1. Domain per [domain-naming.md](domain-naming.md). One canonical host, HTTPS everywhere, HSTS header. Redirects from any old URLs are 301 or 308 and stay for at least one year (Google's site-move guidance).
2. Server-rendered HTML for every indexable page (SSR or SSG). Verify: `curl -sA Googlebot URL | grep -c '<h1'` returns 1.
3. Per page: unique title with the keyword in the first 60 characters, unique meta description, one H1 (convention), self-referencing absolute canonical, viewport meta, `lang` attribute, alt text on meaningful images, `width`/`height` on images, a real `src` on every `<img>`, hero image not lazy-loaded, slug that is the shortened H1. DE/AT/CH variants get reciprocal `hreflang` (`de`, `de-AT`, `de-CH`, `x-default`) only if the pages actually differ; identical text is one page.
4. `robots.txt` allows public paths, allows `OAI-SearchBot`, `PerplexityBot`, `Bingbot`, `Claude-SearchBot`, and `Google-Extended` (citations in ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Copilot, Claude, and Gemini grounding), and lists the sitemap. Training-only tokens (`GPTBot`, `ClaudeBot`, `Applebot-Extended`) are the owner's call. No llms.txt needed: no engine states it reads one. `sitemap.xml` with `lastmod` of the last significant change per URL, never the build time (Google stops trusting inaccurate dates; it ignores `priority` and `changefreq`). Unknown URLs return 404. No redirect chains.
5. `jorekai-seo:tech-audit` with `--crawl`: zero FAIL.
6. Google Search Console: domain property via DNS, submit the sitemap, URL-inspect the five most important pages and request indexing.
7. Bing Webmaster Tools: import from GSC, submit the sitemap. ChatGPT search draws on Bing's index (in one analysis, 87 % of ChatGPT citations sat in Bing's top 20), so Bing is the entry ticket to ChatGPT and Copilot visibility. Enable IndexNow if the platform supports it (Bing, Yandex, Naver, Seznam, Yep, Amazon consume it; Google does not). In Germany Bing is a desktop audience: Statcounter, August 2026, gives it 12.8 % of desktop searches and 0.5 % of mobile (5.8 % overall; Google 88.5 %). B2B sites care, consumer mobile sites less.
8. Analytics: GSC is mandatory. One privacy-friendly web analytics tool for on-site behaviour.
9. Internal linking: every page within three clicks of the home page; every important page has at least one contextual internal link with a descriptive anchor.
10. Legal pages before launch, not by SEO: a commercially run site in Germany needs an imprint (Impressum) with name, address, and fast-contact details (Digitale-Dienste-Gesetz, paragraph 5); a site that collects personal data needs a privacy policy covering what GDPR Article 13 requires the controller to disclose (identity, purpose, legal basis, retention, rights). Google's rater guidelines (September 2025) separately rate pages that take money or trust as Low when contact and customer-service information is missing, so link the imprint, the privacy policy, and a contact page from every page footer.
11. Launch small: 10–30 strong pages, get them indexed, then scale. Tag, category, and filter pages come after the first batch is indexed. Crawl budget is not the reason on a small site (Google says it only matters above roughly 10,000 fast-changing or 1,000,000 pages); the reason is that thin auto-generated pages compete with the real ones and read as scaled low-value content.
